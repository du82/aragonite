# Aragonite<sup>α</sup>

We are developing a privacy-focused search engine with a sovereign index from the ground up, leveraging multi-threaded Rust. Our engine does not log anything or use any JavaScript at all, and makes one response per request.

No to bloat.\
No to glowie.\
No to spyware.\
No to subpoena.\
No! No! No! No!

 ### The 'Why'

Mainstream search engines do not hide the fact they want to push the highest bidder in your face. Google, Bing, DDG, and others are full of ads, SEO spam, listicles, and have a bias problem. As of the time of writing this, Google and Bing are adding to their list of problems by forcing Artificial Intelligence onto their users.

Metasearch engines (Such as Searx/SearXNG) crawl the indexes with these problems, and experience downstream effects when quality degrades in the higher order indexes. Kagi has very high quality results but you have to pay a subscription fee and have limited queries. Mojeek offers some of the privacy features we do, and also has an independent index; we wanted to go a step further. We have faster query returns than Mojeek, offer even better privacy, even stronger security, and the most robust querying options available. Aragonite works on airgapped networks, and even offline!

We have a value proposition no one else brings to the table.

### The Aragonite Search Engine: Features

- No JavaScript or WASM. Fully auditable client-side.
- Robust syntax options. Our filters are hyperspecific.
- Independant index. No to Google! No to DuckFuckGo! No to Whoogle! No to Searx/ng!
- No logs. IP Addresses and Search Queries are stored in RAM. Nothing is stored on disk.
- Indepdendent engine built from the ground up, designed for security, and extensively battle tested it.
- We track privacy and human rights violations of various entities.
- We show you the small web.
- We index the dark web.
- We offer an official Tor site.
- We don't censor. You choose to disable what you don't wish to see.
- We offer distributed mirror instances.
- We have a custom data format based on open standards.
- Minimal dependencies. No reliance on 3rd parties.
