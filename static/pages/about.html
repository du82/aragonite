About Us

Welcome! We are two individuals (one programmer and one project leader) building a search engine from scratch that values your privacy, while providing a programming-style syntax for more accurate results.

Our Promise to You:


  • We will uphold human rights and fiercely protect user privacy by limiting the amount of data that we collect; treating it like toxic waste that needs to be discarded once it's nolonger useful.
  • We will maintain a fully independent index, free from the censorship and bias of big tech.
  • We will make our software fully auditable client-side so that you can know exactly what runs on your machine.
  • We will prioritize child safety and not index websites that promote or distribute child sexual abuse material (CSAM).
  • We will provide mirrors and aggressive proxying to ensure that you can always access the service.
  • We will provide a Tor hidden service and not discriminate against traffic. The only exception being system abuse and scrapers.

  • We will not log your searches or track you in any way.
  • We will not use JavaScript or WebAssembly, as they are both vectors for tracking and malware.
  • We will not use any tracking cookies or fingerprinting techniques.
  • We will not make any attempt to correlate queries with their source.
