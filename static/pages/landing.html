Welcome to Project Obscura

(pre-alpha)

Project Obscura is a fully independent search engine, allowing for hyper-specific queries, custom user themes, and built for privacy conscious individuals.

Written exclusively in Rust/HTML/CSS and extensively tested for security. There's no JavaScript here. Everything that runs on your machine is auditable with the F12 key.

We collect your IP Address and Search Query to provide results. Nothing is stored on the disk.

Use the search bar above to get started!

Helpful Hints:

  • Put a + before words that must be in the results: +cats
  • Put a - before words that must not be in the results: -cats
  • Put category: before a category that you wish to search in: category:forum
  • Put tags: before a tag that must be in the results: tag:linux
  • Put status: before operational or defunct to look for operational or defunct projects: status:defunct
  • Put them all together for hyper-specific results:
    +dread -reddit category:forum tags:opsec already
    returns results that include dread, do not include reddit, are in the "forum" category, have "opsec" in the tags, and also do a normal search for "already"
