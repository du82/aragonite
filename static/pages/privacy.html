PRIVACY POLICY - Last Updated: April 30, 2024

OUR COMMITMENT: We collect minimal data and retain nothing permanently. Your privacy is our priority.

DATA COLLECTION: We temporarily collect only IP Address (stored in RAM for maximum 30 seconds to prevent abuse) and Search Query (stored in RAM until 16KB buffer fills to detect scrapers).

KEY PROTECTIONS: IP addresses and search queries are never linked together. We maintain no permanent storage or logging, create no user profiles or tracking, and collect no personally identifying information.

ANONYMOUS ACCESS: Use our Tor service for complete anonymity.

CHILDREN'S PRIVACY: Since we collect no identifying information, users of all ages are protected equally.

POLICY UPDATES: Changes take effect immediately upon posting. We will explain the reason for any updates.

This policy reflects our commitment to knowing as little about our users as possible.
